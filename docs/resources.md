# Resources

## Verified complaint channels
- Federation of State Medical Boards (directory): https://www.fsmb.org/contact-a-state-medical-board/
- The Joint Commission patient safety event form: https://www.jointcommission.org/en-us/contact-us/report-a-patient-safety-event
- HHS OCR complaint portal: https://ocrportal.hhs.gov/ocr/smartscreen/main.jsf
- CMS state survey agencies: https://www.cms.gov/medicare/health-safety-standards/quality-safety-oversight-general-information/contact-information
- Medicare BFCC-QIO / complaint finder: https://www.medicare.gov/basics/your-medicare-rights/get-help-with-your-coverage/file-a-complaint
- HHS OIG fraud hotline: 1-800-HHS-TIPS (1-800-447-8477) or https://oig.hhs.gov
- AHRQ How to Complain: https://www.ahrq.gov/patients-consumers/patient-involvement/how-to-complain.html

## Key statutes
- 42 CFR 482.13 — Hospital patient rights and grievance process
- HIPAA — 45 CFR Parts 160 & 164
- Section 1557 — non-discrimination in health programs

## Notes
- Joint Commission shreds medical records on receipt — summarize only, never attach PHI.
- OCR privacy complaints have a 180-day window from when you knew of the violation.
- Hospitals must respond in writing under CMS rules.
- Retaliation for filing an OCR complaint is prohibited by law.
